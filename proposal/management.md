## Risk Assessment

- When working in the Dyson Centre, tools or machines may be required which pose a threat via injury:
    - Hand tools pose a low risk at minor unjuries such as bruises or cuts.
        - Mitigate this risk by always working with a partner.
        - Be aware of the nearest first aid kit and technician if assistance is needed.
        - Only ever use a tool for the function it was intended to perform.
    - The heavy machinery such as the guillotine pose high risk if misused.
        - Mitigate this risk by ensuring that the user of the machine is informed on its function and has been through the training if necessary.
        - Be aware of the nearest first aid kit and technician if assistance is needed.
        - Exercise caution when operating the machine, ensuring there is a clear working space around the machine.
        - Ensure the user has knowledge of the location of the emergency stop if the machine is fitted with this.
- The SOLARSAFE concept features heating elements and sinks:
    - Contact with a hot component poses a moderate risk of harm through burns.
        - Minimise this risk by designing a shroud for any such elements and only testing them with the shroud attached.
        - During and after testing, leave the elements in a safe and clear location where it is unlikely to be touched accidentally while it runs or cools.
    - Materials touching the hot component poses a moderate risk of fire or release of hazardous fumes.
        - To minimise this risk, inspect the device for any scraps or offcuts that may have entered and clear them out if found.
        - During and after testing, leave the elements in an exclusion zone where it cannot contact any material in error.
- The SOLARSAFE concept features low power electrical components:
    - Improper construction leading to short circuits poses a moderate risk of damage and harm through burns.
        - To minimise this risk, use a second opinion to assess whether the circuitry has been build according to plan.
        - Clean the circuitry where any exposed contuctor is present before turning on the power.
        - Where possible, include insulators and isolators (fuses, breakers) in the circuit designs.
    - Low risk of electrocution:
        - Mitigate this risk by always working with a partner.
        - Be aware of the nearest first aid kit and technician if assistance is needed.
        - Avoid handling the exposed circuitry when turned on.
        
## Contingencies

- Should the heating approach not work (through energy or other constraints)
    - we could pivot to attempting to improve the even drying of the maize.
- Should the solutions become too expensive
    - we could pivot to using our design to characterise the problem and provided data on design demands and constraints for the future.
- Should the battery/energy demands for operation at night prove too difficult to overcome
    - we could pivot to attempting to minimise drying time in the day in order to increase throughput this way.
