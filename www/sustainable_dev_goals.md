The United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) serve as a universal blueprint for a better, more sustainable future for all. The SolarSafe dehumidifier module directly targets humanitarian, economic, and agricultural challenges faced by smallholder farmers in Sub-Saharan Africa. While the project yields widespread indirect benefits across multiple global goals, its engineering design and core objectives most heavily impact four specific SDGs, alongside strict adherence to UNICEF's Principles for Digital Development.

### SDG 2: Zero Hunger
<img src="www/Images/sdg2.png" width="240" alt="SDG 2">
In Kenya, approximately 70% of cultivated maize is grown specifically for human consumption, making it a staple for national food security. However, improper post-harvest drying often leads to the proliferation of aflatoxin - a highly toxic, carcinogenic fungus that makes crops entirely inedible and dangerous. When contamination strikes, a large percentage of a farmer's yield must be thrown away. By optimizing the SolarSafe maize drying system, our dehumidifier solution accelerates the drying process. Preventing aflatoxin growth ensures a significantly higher volume of safe, consumable maize reaches vulnerable populations, directly reducing the number of malnourished individuals in rural communities.

### SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being
<img src="www/Images/sdg3.png" width="240" alt="SDG 2">
Aflatoxin exposure poses severe public health risks, including acute poisoning, chronic liver damage, and stunting in children. Because the fungus leaves no visible marks and is entirely odourless, detecting contaminated grain at the consumer level is incredibly difficult. Therefore, the most effective preventative healthcare strategy is mitigation during the drying phase before the food enters the supply chain. Our engineering solution successfully lowers moisture levels, reducing the diagnostic and treatment burden on local healthcare infrastructure and directly safeguarding community well-being.

### SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth
<img src="www/Images/sdg8.png" width="240" alt="SDG 2">
Smallholder farmers currently invest physical labour and financial capital into maize cultivation, only to lose a substantial portion of their harvest each year to spoilage. The persistent threat of contamination forces farmers to accept lower market rates from buyers who price in the risk of aflatoxin. The SolarSafe module empowers farmers to meet higher food safety standards. By securing higher-quality, certified yields, farmers achieve superior crop valuations. This not only better supports individual household livelihoods but also provides the financial stability required to reinvest in farming operations, incentivise local employment, and stimulate economic growth in more remote, rural areas.

### SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production
<img src="www/Images/sdg12.png" width="240" alt="SDG 2">
To feed growing global populations sustainably, agricultural efficiency must increase without unsustainably expanding land use or depleting natural resources. Our dehumidifier module aims to maximise the output of consumable food and animal feed from existing land masses dedicated to farming. By shifting from wasteful production to optimised post-harvest preservation, the project minimises agricultural waste and exemplifies responsible resource management.

### Alignment with UNICEF’s Principles for Digital Development

To ensure the long-term viability of the engineering changes made to the SolarSafe system, our design process closely aligned with UNICEF's framework for sustainable deployment:

* **Design with People:** The existing SolarSafe infrastructure is highly modular. Our dehumidifier extension explicitly maintains this modularity, ensuring that the technology remains intuitive, accessible, and easily maintainable for Sub-Saharan smallholder farmers who may not possess advanced technical training.
* **Build for Sustainability:** The product runs entirely off a solar photovoltaic cell using renewable energy. Our development process prioritised maintaining this zero-emission profile to ensure the solution remains environmentally sustainable and free from the burning of organic material.
* **Design for Inclusion:** Because smallholder communities frequently share equipment, the module is engineered for rapid, straightforward deployment, allowing multiple farming families to cycle, share, and utilise the device quickly across a harvest season.
* **Use Evidence to Improve Outcomes:** The chosen solution and the power-split configuration between the system's electrical components were determined through rigorous empirical testing. Our finalised design successfully minimises overall power consumption while maximising moisture extraction and drying performance, ensuring all engineering outcomes are firmly backed by extensive data.

In conclusion, the SolarSafe dehumidifier module successfully bridges clean engineering with socio-economic development, directly uplifting agricultural communities while respecting international framework standards for sustainable, inclusive development.
